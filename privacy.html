Vidikon Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026

Vidikon ("Vidikon," "we," "us," or "our") is a media player for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV that plays television and video-on-demand catalogs from an IPTV provider that you choose and connect yourself. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, share and protect your information when you use the Vidikon app and related services (the "Services"). It is incorporated by reference into our Terms of Use.

The short version. Vidikon does not host, sell or recommend television content. You bring your own provider. To make that work we store your provider login on our servers, encrypted, so that the app can sync your catalog and build playback links on your behalf. We do not run advertising, we do not sell your data, and we do not use analytics trackers. The only telemetry we collect is crash and error reporting so we can fix bugs.

I. Information We Collect

Account information

When you first open Vidikon we create an anonymous account for you. It has no name or email attached; it is a random identifier that lets your profiles, watch progress and lists follow you between your devices. You may optionally upgrade that account by signing in with Apple, Google or Discord. If you do, we receive and store:

  • Email address, as provided by the sign-in provider (with Sign in with Apple you may choose to hide your real address).
  • First and last name, if the sign-in provider shares them.
  • A provider identifier that links your Vidikon account to your Apple, Google or Discord identity so you can sign in again.

We do not store a profile picture and we do not ask for a date of birth, phone number or postal address.

Provider connection data

To play your content, Vidikon needs to talk to your IPTV provider. Depending on what you add, we store:

  • Xtream Codes logins: the server address, username and password of your provider account. The username and password are encrypted before they are written to our database (see Security below).
  • M3U playlist URLs and direct stream URLs that you enter, including any access tokens embedded in those URLs by your provider.
  • Provider account status reported by your provider's API, such as the subscription expiry date, whether the account is a trial, and the maximum number of simultaneous connections.

Catalog and viewing data

  • Catalog listings synced from your provider: channel, movie and series names, categories, artwork links and electronic program guide (EPG) entries. These describe what your provider offers, not what you watched. Listings for a given provider are shared between all Vidikon users who connect to that same provider.
  • Watch progress: the item you were playing and how far into it you were, so you can resume across devices.
  • Lists and reactions: your watchlist and any custom lists, items you liked or disliked, series you dismissed from "continue watching", and which stream you last added by hand.
  • Profile names you give to each provider connection inside the app.

Device and technical data

  • Paired devices: when you link an Apple TV to your account, we store the device's name and platform so you can see and revoke the link.
  • Timezone and language of your device, used to localize the program guide.
  • Crash and error reports: we use Bugsnag to collect crashes and errors from the app and from our server functions. A report includes the app version, device model and operating system version, a stack trace, and a breadcrumb trail of the API requests the app made shortly before the error (request path, duration, HTTP status and a request identifier). Reports do not include your provider password or the contents of what you were watching.
  • Server logs: our hosting providers record the IP address, timestamp and request path of calls made to our servers, as is standard for any internet service, and use the IP address for rate limiting and abuse prevention.

Camera

On iPhone and iPad, Vidikon asks for camera access only when you choose to scan the pairing code shown on your Apple TV. Frames are processed on your device to read the code and are never uploaded or stored.

Downloads

Content you download for offline viewing is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage, excluded from device backups. We keep no server-side record of what you downloaded.

What we do not collect

We do not use advertising identifiers, analytics or attribution SDKs, location services, contacts, photos, microphone, health data or push notifications. We do not track you across other apps or websites.

II. How We Use Information

  • To run the Services: authenticate you, sync and display your provider's catalog, build playback links, keep your watch progress and lists in sync across devices, and pair your Apple TV.
  • To enrich the catalog: we match the titles in your provider's catalog against The Movie Database (TMDB) to show posters, descriptions, cast and ratings. We send TMDB title names and identifiers, never your account or provider details.
  • To keep the Services working: diagnose crashes and errors, monitor performance, and prevent abuse.
  • To communicate with you: respond to support requests sent to us, and notify you of material changes to the Services or these policies.
  • To comply with law: respond to lawful requests, enforce our Terms of Use, and protect the rights and safety of Vidikon and its users.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling or sale to third parties, and we do not make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.

IV. Security

  • Provider credentials are encrypted at rest. Xtream usernames and passwords are encrypted with AES-256 before being stored. The encryption key is held in a separate managed secret store, not alongside the data, so database backups and exports contain only ciphertext.
  • Access is scoped to you. Every row of account, provider and viewing data is protected by database-level access rules so that it can be read or changed only by the account that owns it.
  • Encryption in transit to our servers. All traffic between the app and our servers uses TLS.
  • Traffic to your provider may not be encrypted. Many IPTV providers only offer plain HTTP endpoints. When that is the case, requests to your provider, including your login as part of the stream URL, travel unencrypted between you (or our servers) and the provider. This is a property of the provider's service, not something Vidikon can change. Prefer providers that support HTTPS.
  • Session tokens on your device are stored in the system keychain.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account or provider login has been compromised, change the password with your provider and contact us.

V. Data Retention and Deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. You can delete your account at any time from the app under Profile › Account › Delete Account. When you do:

  • Your email, name and sign-in identities are removed and your login is disabled immediately.
  • Your stored provider usernames and passwords are erased.
  • Your watch progress, lists, reactions, paired devices and hand-added streams are deleted.
  • Your profile records and provider connection records are retained without credentials for a short period for integrity and abuse-prevention purposes, then removed.

Catalog listings synced from a provider are shared reference data and are not tied to your account; they are refreshed and pruned on the provider's sync cycle and remain as long as any user is connected to that provider. Crash reports are retained by Bugsnag according to its retention settings and contain no direct identifiers. Encrypted backups of our database are rotated on a fixed cycle after which deleted data no longer exists in any copy.

Data you downloaded to your device is under your control and is removed when you delete the download or uninstall the app.
